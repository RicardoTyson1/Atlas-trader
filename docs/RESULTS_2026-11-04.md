## Verdict

None of the 12 default strategy/instrument combinations shows an edge.
No combination is positive in both halves with a t-stat near 2+. The few positive
OOS numbers flip sign versus IS, which is noise. Do NOT trade any of these as-is.

Sizing note: at $10k with 0.5% risk ($50), index CFDs (lotSize 100 → $1/pt at
0.01 lot) are often too coarse. A 60-point NAS100 stop at the minimum 0.01 lot
already risks ~$60, so many index trades are skipped. Gold sizes fine.
